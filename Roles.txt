7. CLUB CODE OF CONDUCT & ETHICAL CREED
================================================================================

Every appointed member of Cyber Shield HPS must abide by the following creed:

1. DEFENSE ONLY (WHITE-HAT PRINCIPLE):
   Knowledge acquired through the club must NEVER be used to breach, bypass,
   disrupt, prank, or inspect systems belonging to the school, classmates,
   or any third party without explicit, documented legal authorization.

2. ZERO VICTIM BLAMING:
   If a classmate or student falls victim to a scam or online harassment, members
   must offer empathetic support, strict confidentiality, and guide them directly
   to a trusted teacher mentor or Helpline 1930.

3. OFFICIAL ESCALATION PATH:
   In case of severe threats (blackmail, extortion, impersonation, or deepfakes),
   members must immediately escalate the incident to the Teacher In-Charge and
   School Management for formal reporting via cybercrime.gov.in.
